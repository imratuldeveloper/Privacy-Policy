Privacy Policy for CampusMate BD

Last updated: October 2026

CampusMate BD is a student companion app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using CampusMate BD, you agree to this policy.

CampusMate BD is not the official app of National University (NU) and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to National University. NU notices shown in the app are collected from the university's public notice page (nu.ac.bd) for students' convenience.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you create an account or log in, we collect your name, mobile number, and email address.
  • Profile information you provide: Course, department, year, institution, and a profile photo if you choose to set one.
  • App data you create: Your notes, bookmarks, download list, budget entries, and book market posts, including book photos you upload.
  • Contact, report, and account-deletion requests: Your name, email, message, reason, and app rating (if you provide one) when you use the in-app Contact form, report content, or request account deletion.
  • Push notification token: If you allow notifications, a device push token is stored only so we can deliver notifications to your device.

Your password is never stored as plain text. It is verified using secure hashing, and we cannot see your password.

We do not collect your location, contacts, SMS messages, or call history. The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code or a document, and photos are uploaded only when you choose to submit them, for example in a book post or as a profile photo.

How We Use Information

Your information is used only to provide the app's services: verifying login, showing your notes and lists on any device you log in on, sorting resources by department, showing notices and library resources, delivering notifications you have allowed, and connecting buyers and sellers in the book market. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

CampusMate BD uses secure cloud services to operate. Your data is stored on a secure cloud backend for accounts, notes, posts, and messages, and notifications are delivered through a push notification service. These services process data only to provide the app's features, as described in their own privacy policies. Public notices are collected from the university's public notice page, and no personal information is sent to that website. Some library files are hosted on an external file service, and opening such a file is subject to that service's privacy policy.

Book Market Information

In a book sale post, your name, area, book photo, and profile photo may be shown to other users. Your phone number is not shown in full in listings. You can delete your post at any time, and a post stops showing in the app after 30 days.

Your Notes and Privacy

Your private notes are visible only to you. They are not shown to other users, and the app administrator cannot read another user's private notes. If you are logged in, your notes are stored securely on the server so you can access them from another device. Notes written without login stay on that device, and are added to your account when you later log in.

Data Security

Data is stored on secure cloud servers, and server-side rules are used so each user can only access their own private data. Still, no method on the internet is 100% secure. Please do not enter sensitive financial information anywhere in the app.

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the service. You can change or delete your profile information at any time, and you can delete your own book posts at any time.

Your Rights and Account Deletion

To permanently delete your account and all of its data, use the Delete account button at the bottom of the Account page inside the app and submit the deletion request form. Your request does not delete the account instantly in the app. After your identity is verified, your account and its data will be permanently deleted, and deleted notes and posts cannot be recovered. Uninstalling the app also removes data stored only on that device.

Children

CampusMate BD is intended for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe such information has been provided, contact us through the in-app Contact form so it can be removed.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will show when it was last updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or wish to request access, correction, or deletion of your data, contact us through the Contact section inside the CampusMate BD app (Profile > Help > Contact).
